# E3.0 diagnosis: GFN2/NO2 third derivatives

E3.0 remains a diagnosis-only package. The evidence separates sampled raw
maps, individual outputs and intermediates of the map-8 Anderson transition,
fixed-count map composition, adaptive convergence selection, potential
finalization, and public-property composition. The new small-step checks do
not support the earlier claim that the map-8 Anderson transition first
becomes third-order inconsistent. The earliest measured reverse/JVP
first-derivative gap is in the map-8 raw residual projection, but its third
response stays within finite-difference uncertainty; the historical
third-order onset remains unresolved. Fresh-call variation is traced to a
tiny map-1 forward perturbation amplified later in the trajectory, while the
specific operation creating that perturbation remains unidentified.
No numerical, SCF, reference, tolerance, or `KNOWN_WRONG` change is included,
and E3.1 has not started.

## Diagnosis and validation

The evidence supports this current localization:

```text
map 8: first small reverse/JVP difference appears in raw residual projection;
    nested third responses remain within derivative-level FD uncertainty
map 10: nested thirds approach small-step FD for all recorded transition
    sub-operations; theta is the first with a relative JVP/reverse gap > 1e-6
map 9 at actual propagated tangent: tested density derivatives agree with FD
fixed-damping Simple map composition: tested composition is internally consistent
Anderson solve conditioning: ill-conditioned; no unique defective operation identified
fixed N=20 / historical tolerance: coarse-step comparison disagrees; not a localizer
fixed N=24 / tighter convergence: endpoint responses approach derivative-level FD
potential finalization: preserves/amplifies the existing mismatch
public property composition: reproduces the historical endpoint failure
```

The map-8 probes distinguish the immediate map-9 input (`current`) from
`x_hist` and `f_hist`, and expose each Anderson intermediate. The first
measurable first-mode discrepancy is a small reverse/JVP difference in the
map-8 `raw` residual projection, before the Anderson solve; its nested third
response remains within the measured FD uncertainty. No map-8 transition
output is established as the source of the historical third-order error.
The map-10 split gives the same caution: `theta` is the first sub-operation
with a relative first-mode JVP/reverse gap above `1e-6`, yet its third
derivative-level FD sequence approaches the nested result. The actual map-9
full-trajectory probe and the propagated-tangent probe pass their tested
projections, but neither proves every map output/order correct. Thus the
first material third-order failure in the complete trajectory remains
unresolved for both historical observables.

Fresh-calculator call-order variation is localized to a quantified upstream
trajectory perturbation, though not to a unique originating kernel. With
OMP/MKL/OpenBLAS thread counts set to one (`torch.get_num_threads() == 1`,
`torch.get_num_interop_threads() == 128`), repeated fresh endpoint calls in
the trajectory/gauge probe produced hyperpolarizabilities
`(33.81128368, 33.79549880, 33.79774462)`, spread `0.0157849`. The first
bitwise forward difference in fresh fixed-20-map traces was map 1's
`mapped_x`, `1.39e-17`; the first gauge-invariant difference was map 6's
Anderson `theta`, `4.92e-12`, and the largest later gauge-invariant
difference was map 20's `theta`, `2.84e-5`. Coefficients diagnostically
flip/rotate within the close occupied pair (coefficient max difference about
`1.33` at map 7), while S-overlap principal angles remain below
`5.4e-8` radians and density/Hamiltonian/energy differences stay below about
