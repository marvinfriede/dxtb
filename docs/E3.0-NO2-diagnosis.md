# E3.0 diagnosis: GFN2/NO2 third derivatives

E3.0 is complete as a diagnosis-only package. The four historical GFN2/NO2
endpoints reproduce, and the first failing layer is recorded as the complete
finite-tolerance fixed-point response / finite-root trajectory. Tightening
root convergence and increasing the effective trajectory move both
hyperpolarizability and `pol_deriv` toward the stable physical finite-
difference and tblite values. The evidence does not identify a defective
Anderson sub-operation, and the report does not infer one from reverse/JVP
gaps that compared different spectral branches. Call-order variation begins
with a map-1 initial-guess difference of order `1e-18` and is strongly
amplified in hidden Anderson state; its originating floating-point instruction
is not a package-closure requirement. No numerical, SCF, reference, tolerance,
or `KNOWN_WRONG` change is included. E3.1 has not started.

Diagnosis therefore closes at the complete finite-tolerance fixed-point
response / finite-root trajectory layer. E3.0 does not claim that one

The previous reverse/JVP gap localization is withdrawn. Forced-branch probes
show that the actual Calculator hyperpolarizability, Calculator `pol_deriv`,
and functional reverse-mode raw maps use `broadening_method="lorn"`, whereas
the functional raw-map JVP path uses `None` under the current transitional
selector. Consequently reverse/JVP differences from those production paths
compare different derivative branches and are not evidence against Anderson.
Within each explicitly forced frozen spectral branch, reverse and JVP are
compared on the same function; the ordinary forward finite difference is
computed only from the ordinary branch. Forward-value equality between the
two branches remains a sanity check, not derivative evidence.

Fresh-process controls separate three sequences. In a clean process with
`torch.set_num_threads(1)` and `torch.set_num_interop_threads(1)`, fixed-N
trace A/A/A is compared without endpoint or unrelated work. A second clean

application state or reproducible isolated spectral/Anderson call-order
variation was found. The experiment intentionally stops at the stage-level
source; identifying the CPU instruction behind an order-`1e-18` initial-guess
rounding difference is not required to close E3.0.
